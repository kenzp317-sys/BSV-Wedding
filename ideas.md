# BSV Wedding Event Website — Design Brainstorm

## Context
A wedding celebration and friends & family gathering at Borgo San Vincenzo, a boutique hotel in the heart of Tuscany, May 24–29, 2027. Visitors from US, Italy, and Taiwan.

## Idea 1: Tuscan Terracotta & Ink

**Design Movement:** Italian Editorial / Architectural Digest Mediterranean

**Core Principles:**
- Warm terracotta and aged parchment tones evoke the sun-baked Tuscan earth
- Generous whitespace punctuated by full-bleed photography creates editorial gravitas
- Asymmetric layouts where text and imagery counterbalance each other
- Serif typography with strong contrast between display and body weights

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: warm off-white parchment (#F5EFE0)
- Primary accent: deep terracotta (#B5541A)
- Secondary: olive green (#6B7A3A)
- Text: near-black ink (#1C1A17)
- Gold highlight: (#C9A84C)
- Emotional intent: warmth, antiquity, celebration, the earth of Tuscany

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Horizontal split sections: large image on one side, text on the other
- Full-bleed hero with text overlay on left third
- Staggered card grids for experiences and attractions
- Sticky side-navigation for long-scroll pages

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin horizontal rules with small decorative diamond separators
- Handwritten-style script font for section labels and pull quotes
- Subtle paper texture overlay on sections

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Smooth parallax scrolling on hero images
- Cards lift with a subtle shadow on hover
- Navigation items underline with a terracotta sweep

**Animation:**
- Fade-up entrance animations for text blocks
- Slow Ken Burns effect on hero images
- Staggered card reveals on scroll

**Typography System:**
- Display: Cormorant Garamond (elegant, editorial serif)
- Body: Lato (clean, readable)
- Accent/Label: Playfair Display Italic for pull quotes
</text>

## Idea 2: Vineyard Modernist

**Design Movement:** Contemporary Italian Luxury / Kinfolk Magazine Aesthetic

**Core Principles:**
- Muted, earthy palette with high-contrast black typography
- Oversized typography as a visual element, not just content
- Generous negative space — sections breathe like a Tuscan afternoon
- Photography-first design where images carry the emotional weight

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: warm white (#FAFAF7)
- Primary: deep forest green (#2D4A2D)
- Accent: dusty rose (#D4A5A0)
- Text: charcoal (#2A2A2A)
- Highlight: warm gold (#C8A96E)
- Emotional intent: sophistication, romance, nature, celebration

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Oversized hero with full-bleed image and minimal text
- Horizontal scrolling gallery sections
- Alternating text-left / image-right sections
- Masonry photo grid for gallery

**Signature Elements:**
- Large decorative numerals (01, 02, 03) as section markers
- Thin vertical lines as dividers
- Circular image crops for portraits and intimate moments

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Cursor changes to a custom ring on hover over images
- Navigation fades in on scroll-up
- Smooth page transitions

**Animation:**
- Reveal animations triggered by scroll intersection
- Image zoom on hover
- Text character-by-character entrance for hero headlines

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display (classic, romantic)
- Body: Source Serif Pro (readable, editorial)
- Labels: Montserrat Light (clean contrast)

## Selected Design: Idea 1 — Tuscan Terracotta & Ink

This approach best captures the warmth and character of Borgo San Vincenzo — the sun-baked terracotta walls, the aged stone, the golden light of Tuscany in May. The editorial layout gives the site a premium, travel-magazine feel appropriate for international guests from the US, Italy, and Taiwan. The Cormorant Garamond / Lato pairing provides elegance without pretension.
